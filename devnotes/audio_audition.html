ENPA — audio audition

Native players, no JavaScript. Right-click a player and enable loop to check the seam.

Judging the music: let each candidate play through 3–4 times, not once. A loop that sounds fine on the first pass can grate badly on the fourth — that is the thing worth testing. Longest loops (D 29s, C 27s) repeat least noticeably.
