A Non-Profit Organization (NPO) account refers to the accounting system used by organizations that operate to provide services and benefits to the public or their members — not to earn profit.

These include charities, clubs, religious institutions, educational societies, and NGOs. Their main purpose is to record financial transactions accurately, ensure transparency, and show how funds such as donations, grants, and membership fees are utilized.

Unlike businesses, NPOs do not distribute profits to owners. Any surplus is reinvested into the organization to support its activities.

1

Receipts & Payments Account

Records all cash received and paid during a specific period. It is a summary of the cash book — includes both capital and revenue items.

2

Subscriptions Account

A ledger account to calculate the subscription income for the current year, adjusting for prepaid and accrued amounts.

3

Trading Account

Prepared for clubs with trading activities (e.g. canteen, tuck shop). Shows gross profit from sales of goods.

4

Income & Expenditure Account

The NPO equivalent of Profit & Loss. Shows all revenue income and expenses to determine Surplus or Deficit.

5

Statement of Financial Position

Shows assets, liabilities and Accumulated Fund (NPO equivalent of Capital/Owner's Equity) at a point in time.
